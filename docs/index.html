Your tabs, organized
as you browse.

TabFlow Agent stops duplicate tabs, groups related tabs by topic, and finds any open, closed or archived tab from one shortcut. It runs on your device and never closes anything you can't get back.

No more duplicates

Already open? You're switched to it.

Open a page you already have open and TabFlow takes you to the existing tab instead of adding another one. A notice with Undo appears, so you stay in control.

  • Only newly opened tabs are affected; browsing inside a tab is never interrupted.
  • Different pages on the same site stay separate, unless you mark the site as one tab only.
  • "Close Duplicate Tabs" shows exactly which tabs will close first, and archives them.

Tabs that organize themselves

See the groups before anything changes.

"Organize All Tabs" proposes tab groups by topic, like Travel, Development or News. Rename any group, then apply. Nothing moves until you say so.

  • New tabs you open later join a matching group automatically (you can turn this off).
  • "Organize Ungrouped Tabs" leaves the groups you made yourself alone.
  • Pull tabs from every window into one with "Consolidate All Windows".

One shortcut for everything

Find any tab. Run any command.

Press Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘+Shift+Space on Mac) or click the toolbar icon to search every open, recently closed and archived tab. Type > for commands.

  • Fully keyboard driven, in light or dark mode.
  • The toolbar icon shows your tab count at a glance.

Built so you never lose a tab

↶

Undo anything

Every change TabFlow makes can be undone: from the notice, from the top of the palette, or with Alt+Shift+U.

📦

Archive, don't lose

Archive tabs you haven't used in a few days (you choose how many), by hand or automatically. They stay searchable and restorable for 90 days.

Private by default

Your tabs stay on your device.

Grouping and duplicate detection run in your browser with a small open-source AI model (about 25 MB, downloaded once and cached). No account, no tracking, no data collected by us.

Want sharper group names? Optionally bring your own AI key. Tab titles and addresses then go to that provider only, and only for the features you use.

Optional

Bring your own AI key

TabFlow works fully without one. Add a key from a provider you already use and it takes over the parts it's good at: smarter group names, and judging whether two different pages on the same site belong in one tab. You pay that provider directly for the tokens you use; TabFlow never sees or resells them.

  1. 1

    Open Settings

    Press Ctrl+Shift+Space, type >, choose Settings.

  2. 2

    Paste your key

    TabFlow recognizes the provider from the key and fills in its endpoint and a fast default model.

  3. 3

    Optionally split engines

    Use one model for grouping and a cheaper, faster one for duplicates, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

  • Keys are stored only in your browser's extension storage. Remove one in Settings and remote calls stop immediately.
  • Only tab titles and addresses are sent, never page content, and only to the provider you chose.
  • Settings → Activity Log shows each AI decision with token counts, so you can see what you're spending.
  • If a call fails or times out, TabFlow falls back to the on-device AI for grouping and leaves your tabs alone.
