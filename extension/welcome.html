TabFlow Agent is ready

Your tabs, organized as you browse. Everything runs on this device unless you choose to add your own AI key.

1

Open the command palette

Press … on any page, or click the TabFlow icon in your toolbar. Search every open, closed and archived tab, or type > for commands.

Your browser could not set this shortcut, usually because another app already uses it. Choose a different one below.

Tip: pin TabFlow to the toolbar from the extensions (puzzle piece) menu so it is always one click away.

2

Organize this window

See how TabFlow would group the tabs in this window. Nothing changes until you apply, and you can undo it afterwards.

Enter apply · Esc back
3

Then just browse

  • No more duplicates. Open a page you already have open and TabFlow switches to that tab instead.
  • Tabs find their group. New tabs join a matching group, by site or by topic.
  • Undo anything. Every change shows an Undo button, or press Alt+Shift+U.
  • Nothing is lost. Tabs TabFlow closes are archived and stay searchable for 90 days.

Private by default

Grouping and duplicate detection use a small AI model that runs in your browser. Tab titles and addresses only leave your device if you add your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key in Settings, and then only go to that provider.
